    Spiral 7 Rules

    Two players, four pieces each. This prototype uses a custom 7 x 7 spiral variant with opposite starting squares.

    • Four fair binary shells give 1, 2, 3, 4 mouth-up faces; zero mouth-up faces count as 8.
    • A qualifying entry roll places a reserve piece on its start square. It does not advance that piece further.
    • One piece moves per roll. Pieces follow their own rotated 48-square route; an exact roll is required to reach the center.
    • Marked safe squares protect all pieces. On other squares, landing captures all opposing pieces there; stacking provides no additional protection.
    • A roll of 4 or 8, or a capture, grants another turn. A bonus roll still grants a turn when no move is available.
    • No capture is required to enter the inner track. First player to bring all four pieces home wins.

    Experiments stop at their configured turn limit and record unfinished games separately. Physical shell bias and other regional rule variants are outside this version.
