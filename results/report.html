Interpretation

The benchmark contains 50 curated legal positions in ten tactical categories. It includes forced-action and immediate-win checks, but is not a representative random sample of reachable gameplay states. Tournament rates depend on the opponent mix, entry rule, horizon and seeds. The budget study holds the greedy rollout policy and eight-turn cutoff constant. Increasing the sample count need not improve performance against this opponent when rollout or cutoff bias dominates.

Feature ablations disable one weight at a time without retuning. The studies use a fixed heuristic opponent with swapped starting positions. Use a larger held-out configuration before reporting comparative performance on a resume or in a research claim.
